FAQ

Questions, answered

The things people ask us most. Can't see yours? Ask us and we'll add it.

The basics

What is Owner Builder in a Box?

An app that keeps an owner builder's paperwork in one place: approvals, insurance, inspections, trades, contracts, neighbour notices, asset protection and dilapidation reports. It tells you what's due next so nothing slips. See all the features.

Who is it for?

People building or renovating their own home as an owner builder. If you're managing the trades yourself instead of hiring a builder, it's for you.

Can I use it now?

Not yet. We're building it with owner builders. In the meantime you can try the demo portal with a sample build. Join the waitlist to try it early and help shape it. The step-by-step guides are free to use right now.

How much will it cost?

We haven't set pricing yet. People on the waitlist will hear first.

Which states does it cover?

Victoria first, because that's where our first checklists come from. Other states will follow, and the guides note where rules differ.

Is this legal or building advice?

No. Owner Builder in a Box helps you organise and keep track. Your building surveyor, your council and your state regulator are the people to check requirements with.

Neighbours, trades and your surveyor

Do my neighbours need to download anything?

No. They scan the QR code on your letterbox notice, see what's coming up, and choose whether to get texts or emails. Anyone who'd rather not sign up still gets a printed notice. How neighbour notices work.

Do my trades need an account?

No. You can upload their licence and insurance yourself. If they use Tradies Bureau, they can share their up-to-date paperwork with you in one link.

Can my building surveyor see my documents?

Only if you share them. You choose which folders, the link is read-only, and it switches off after 7 days or whenever you turn it off. See what they'd see.

Your records

Who owns my documents and photos?

You do. The plan is that you can download your whole build file (every permit, certificate, contract and photo, sorted into folders) any time, including after your build is finished.

What happens to my neighbours' details?

Neighbours only give their details if they choose to. They're used for updates about your build, never shared or used for marketing, and every message has an unsubscribe link. See our privacy page.

Will it work on my phone on site?

Yes. It's designed for a phone first: snapping documents, ticking off letterboxes and booking inspections all happen on site.

Does this website track me?

We count page visits so we know which pages are useful, using a service that sets no cookies and collects no personal information. If your browser asks not to be tracked, we don't count you. More on our privacy page.

What's the connection with Tradies Bureau?

Owner Builder in a Box is a Tradies Bureau product. Tradies Bureau makes compliance tools for trade businesses, and the two link up so trades can share their paperwork with owner builders.
