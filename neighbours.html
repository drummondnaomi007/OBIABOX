Neighbour notifications

Tell the whole street in one tap

You don't have your neighbours' numbers, and you shouldn't need to knock on nine doors every time a concrete truck is coming. Do one letterbox drop, and after that every update goes out from your phone.

See the sample notice See a neighbour's view

How it works

1

Set up the project

Your address, site hours, a contact number and the key dates: demolition, excavation, crane day, slab pour, any road or footpath closures.

2

Print and letterbox the notice

The app makes a one-page notice with a QR code that's unique to your project. Drop it at each nearby house, and tick them off on a map as you go.

3

Neighbours opt in

The QR code opens your project's neighbour page. It shows what's coming up and who to call, and neighbours can sign up for SMS or email alerts.

4

Post an update, everyone's told

Add an activity and the app sends it to everyone who opted in. It also makes a fresh printable notice for anyone who prefers paper.

5

It's all on the record

Every notice and update is saved with the date and the number of people it went to. If a complaint goes to council, you can show what you sent and when.

Built to be fair to neighbours

Opt-in only

Neighbours give their own details, choose how they hear from you, and can unsubscribe in every message.

No spam, ever

Updates are about the build only. Neighbour details are never shared, sold or used for marketing.

Paper still counts

Not everyone wants a text. Every update can also be printed for the letterbox.
