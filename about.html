About

Owner building without the paperwork panic

Owner builders take on a builder's legal responsibilities without a builder's office staff. We want them to have the same kind of systems a good builder has.

Where it came from

Owner Builder in a Box comes from Tradies Bureau, which builds simple compliance tools for trade businesses. Owner builders kept running into the same problems the trades do (expiring documents, missed inspections, nothing written down), plus a few of their own: neighbours, council bonds and dilapidation reports.

What we believe

  • Owner builders should get clear help, not condescension.
  • Good records protect everyone: you, your neighbours and your trades.
  • Telling people early beats sorting out complaints later.
  • Keep it simple. If it needs a manual, we've built it wrong.

Not legal or building advice

Owner Builder in a Box helps you organise and keep track. It doesn't replace your relevant building surveyor, your council or your state regulator. Always check the requirements for your project with them.
