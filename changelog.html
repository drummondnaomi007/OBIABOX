Version history

The website and the app each have a version number. When they match, what you read here is what the app does. The footer on every page shows both, and warns you if they're out of sync.

Website v0.7.0 · App v0.7.0

2026-10-17

  • The whole website now has a dark mode that follows your device setting, like the demo portal.
  • Buttons and links are easier to tap on phones (at least 44 pixels tall).
  • Cleaner pages: no more labels stacked above headings, simple line icons instead of emoji, and calmer tip boxes.
  • Headings run in order on every page, so screen readers can jump through them.
  • Long paragraphs wrap sooner so they're easier to read, and the build tracker text is bigger.

Website v0.6.2 · App v0.6.1

2026-10-17

  • The demo portal now has a dark mode that follows your device setting.
  • Reset demo asks you to confirm on the page instead of in a pop-up.
  • The demo portal can now be shared as a single page on claude.ai, where reviewers can comment on any screen.

Website v0.6.1 · App v0.6.0

2026-10-17

  • New: a working demo of the OBIABOX portal for review. Sign in to a sample build (or start your own), then use every screen: dashboard, compliance, inspections, trades and contracts, neighbours, asset protection, dilapidation, documents, and sharing.
  • Reviewers can leave notes on any screen and copy or download them.
  • Demo data is saved only in your browser, and Reset demo starts again.

Website v0.6.0 · App v0.5.0

2026-10-16

  • Waitlist form ready for Tally (free), with a form-service option and the email fallback kept as a backup.
  • Visitor stats ready with Cloudflare Web Analytics: page counts only, no cookies, and anyone who asks not to be tracked isn't counted.
  • Privacy page and FAQ updated to say exactly what's collected.

Website v0.5.0 · App v0.5.0

2026-10-16

  • Version numbers for the website and the app, shown in every footer, with a warning when they're out of sync.
  • New FAQ, privacy and What's new pages, plus a friendly page for broken links.
  • Easier to use with a keyboard and screen reader, and fixes for very small screens.
  • Everything so far: features, neighbour notices, eight step-by-step guides, and eleven app mockups (dashboard, neighbour updates, asset protection, dilapidation map, trades register, quotes, contracts, inspections, document vault, letterbox drop and the surveyor's view).
