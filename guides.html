Guides

Owner building, one small job at a time

There are a lot of moving parts. These guides break them into small jobs you can tick off, each taking anywhere from ten minutes to an afternoon. Do them in order, or jump to the one you need today.

Tick steps as you go. Your ticks are saved on this device. Hit Print on any guide to stick it on the fridge or the site shed wall.

Guide 1 · Start here · 15 minutes

Set up your build

You don't need everything on day one. Get the basics in and the app will nag you about the rest. Nicely.

Top tip: Don't wait for perfect. A rough date beats no date. Reminders only work if there's something to remind you about.

Guide 2 · Before you build

Get your approvals in order

The paperwork bit. Boring? Yes. But it's also the bit that can stop your build cold if it's missing.

NOTE: Rules change between states and councils, and they change over time. Your council and building surveyor are the people to check with. OBIAB keeps track for you. It doesn't give legal advice.

Guide 3 · Quotes, checks, contracts

Hire a trade without the headaches

Picture three quotes on the kitchen bench. One's thousands cheaper. You're about to ring them, until you spot it's labour only and there's no insurance on file. Same plans and the same scope for everyone saves you that surprise.

Top tip: The cheapest quote is often the one that left something out. Read the "excludes" line twice.

Guide 4 · One afternoon, the week before works

Before anyone starts digging

This is your chance to protect your money and keep the neighbours on side. It takes an afternoon, and it pays for itself.

Here's the kicker: those dated photos are often the difference between getting your bond back and arguing about a crack that was there before you started.

Guide 5 · Every week on site · 10 minutes

Your 10-minute Monday check

A builder has an office doing this. You have Monday morning and a coffee. That's enough.

Top tip: Make it a repeating calendar event. Same time, every week. The habit is what keeps the stress down. Clear the ticks each Monday and start fresh.

Guide 6 · Each inspection stage

Pass your inspection first time

Inspections fail for silly reasons more often than you'd think. A missing certificate. A wall lined a day too early. A five-minute ready check stops that.

NOTE: Never cover up work (lining walls, pouring concrete, backfilling trenches) until the inspection for that stage has passed.

Guide 7 · Whenever money moves

Changes and payments, the calm way

Most money arguments on a build start with "but you said…". Write it down and nobody has to remember.

Top tip: If a bill turns up for a change you never approved, you've got the record. Calm, polite, sorted.

Guide 8 · Handover

Finish strong

The last ten per cent always feels endless. This gets you over the line with nothing left sitting in a drawer.

NOTE: If you sell within a few years of finishing, there can be extra steps. In Victoria, for example, owner builders may need a defects report and insurance before they sell. Keep everything, and check the rules for your state.
