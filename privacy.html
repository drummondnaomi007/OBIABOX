Privacy

Your information, in plain English

Short version: we only collect what we need, we don't sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

Draft: this page will be reviewed before the app launches. It describes this website today and how we plan to handle information in the app.

This website

  • Visit counts, not people. We count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics so we know which pages help. It uses no cookies and doesn't collect personal information or follow you to other sites. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, we don't count you at all. There are no advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • Saved on your device only. Your house-building progress and your ticks on the guides are saved in your own browser. They never leave your device. Clear your browser's site data to remove them.
  • Waitlist. When you join the waitlist, your answers are stored securely by our form provider, Tally, and sent to us. If you email us instead, we receive whatever you choose to send. We use it only to tell you about Owner Builder in a Box, and never sell or share it. Ask us any time and we'll remove you.
  • Demos. The mockups and sample pages don't send or store anything you type.

The app (when it launches)

  • Your build records belong to you. You'll be able to download everything and close your account.
  • Neighbours opt in. We only hold a neighbour's details if they sign up themselves. We use them for updates about that build only, and every message has an unsubscribe link.
  • You choose who sees what. Shared links (for example, to your building surveyor) are read-only, cover only the folders you pick, and expire.
  • We don't sell your information. Not to trades, suppliers or anyone else.

Questions or requests

Email hello@tradiesbureau.com to ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted.
