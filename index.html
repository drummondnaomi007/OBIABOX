What's in the box

The things that cause owner builders the most stress, kept in one tidy place.

Compliance tracker

Certificate of Consent, White Card, building permit, insurance and inspection stages, with reminders before anything is due.

Neighbour notifications

Print one letterbox notice with a QR code. Neighbours opt in, and you send updates about noisy or disruptive days with one tap.

Asset protection & dilapidation

Before photos, bond receipts and dilapidation reports for every neighbouring property, dated and ready if there's ever a dispute.

Trades, quotes & contracts

Check every trade's compliance, compare quotes side by side, and keep each contract, variation and payment in order.

How it works

Set up your project once. After that, the app tells you what's next.

1

Add your project

Address, council, project type and rough dates. We build your checklist from that.

2

Drop in your documents

Permits, policies, reports and photos all go in the vault, each with its key dates.

3

Keep the neighbours in the loop

Letterbox the notice once. After that, every update is one tap, and every send is logged.
