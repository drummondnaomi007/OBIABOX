What's in the box

Five tools that share one project, one document vault and one reminder system, so nothing slips between them.

How neighbour notices work See app mockups
1 · Compliance tracker

You're the builder, so it's your paperwork

This is the Tradies Bureau Compliance Tracker, set up for the things an owner builder is responsible for.

📄

Approvals

Certificate of Consent (Victoria, for domestic work over $16,000), planning permit, building permit and any permit conditions.

🎓

Your tickets

White Card, owner builder course or eLearning assessment, and the expiry dates that matter.

🛡

Insurance

Construction and public liability cover, plus a reminder of your warranty obligations if you sell within the statutory period.

🔍

Inspection stages

Footings, slab, frame and final inspections. You get a nudge to book each one before the work it checks gets covered up. See the booking mockup.

👷

Your trades

Every trade's licence, insurance and WorkCover checked before they start. Quotes and contracts are covered below.

🗂

Document vault

Everything you'll need at handover, at sale or in a dispute, saved in one place and dated.

2 · Neighbour notifications

Keep the street on side

Most neighbour complaints start with a surprise: a crane, a concrete truck, a 7am angle grinder. Tell people first and most of those complaints never happen.

One letterbox drop

Print a one-page notice with your project details, site hours, a contact number and a QR code. See the sample notice.

Neighbours opt in

The QR code opens your project's neighbour page. Neighbours choose SMS or email updates. They're never added without asking. See the sample page.

One tap, logged

Add "Slab pour Tue 7am, trucks on street" and everyone who opted in is told. Every send is dated and saved in case you need to show you gave notice.

3 · Asset protection

Get your bond back

Many councils need an asset protection permit and bond before works start. What gets your bond back is proof of what the footpath, kerb and nature strip looked like on day one.

Before works

  • Permit, conditions and bond receipt stored together
  • Guided photo checklist: footpath, kerb, crossover, nature strip, street trees, pits
  • Photos are dated automatically and can't be edited later

After works

  • Reminder to book the final asset inspection
  • Before and after photos side by side
  • Bond refund tracked until the money's back

Permit names, bond amounts and timing differ between councils. Your checklist starts from your council's rules and you can edit it.

5 · Trades, quotes & contracts

You're the builder, so you manage the trades too

Without a builder, every trade is your own contract. OBIAB keeps the paperwork a builder's office would keep: who's compliant, who quoted what, what you agreed, and what you've paid.

🛡

Trade compliance

Licence or registration, public liability, WorkCover and ABN for every trade, with reminders before anything lapses. Trades on Tradies Bureau share their live status with one link.

At the end of each job, collect their compliance certificates (plumbing, electrical, waterproofing) before the final payment.

⚖

Tendering & quotes

Bundle the plans and scope for each work package, invite three or more trades, and compare quotes side by side: price, what's included and excluded, start date, and compliance.

Award the job and the quote becomes the start of the contract record.

📑

Contract admin

One record per trade: the signed contract, a payment schedule tied to stages, and variations approved in writing before work starts.

Payments can wait for the matching inspection to pass, and your budget updates as you go.

See the trades mockups

Depending on the work and its value, trades contracting directly with you may need to be licensed or registered, and contract rules vary by state. Check with your building regulator or consumer affairs office.
